SEVEN DAYS AT THE MOUNTAIN
Mountain of the Lord Prayer Center, Katoloni

Day 1: Thanksgiving
Read Psalm 100. Thank God for His goodness and faithfulness.

Day 2: Surrender
Read Romans 12:1-2. Place every plan and concern before the Lord.

Day 3: Wisdom
Read James 1:5. Ask God for wisdom in your home, work and decisions.

Day 4: Family
Read Joshua 24:15. Pray for salvation, peace and unity in every home.

Day 5: The Church
Read Acts 2:42-47. Pray for the church to grow in truth, love and service.

Day 6: Katoloni
Read Jeremiah 29:7. Pray for our neighbours, leaders, schools and families.

Day 7: Renewal
Read Isaiah 40:28-31. Wait on the Lord and receive new strength.
